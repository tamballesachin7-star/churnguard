The Problem

Silent Customer Churn

Most subscription and SaaS companies discover customer dissatisfaction only after cancellation or non-renewal notice arrives. Traditional CRM tools report historical churn rather than detecting the gradual activity decline and license dormancy that precedes cancellation by 30 to 60 days.

The Solution

Predictive Early Warning Engine

ChurnGuard AI monitors telemetry velocity, license seat utilization, and support signals to predict cancellation probability before renewal. It provides root-cause explainability ("Why are they leaving?") and triggers automated retention playbooks (QBR invites, adoption workshops, custom discounts).

Key Technical Innovations

Under the hood of the ChurnGuard platform

1

Universal Schema Inference

Users can drag-and-drop any company's CSV. The engine dynamically detects customer identifiers, churn labels, revenue columns, and behavioral signals regardless of varying column naming conventions.

2

AutoML & Risk Explainability

Trains an ensemble of Gradient Boosting and Random Forest models with automated feature scaling. Calculates per-client feature deviations to deliver concrete bulleted reasons for each at-risk client.

3

Actionable Retention Playbooks

Bridges predictive AI to business action by mapping specific risk drivers to automated interventions (CSM outreach, Tier-3 support escalations, multi-year renewal incentives).
