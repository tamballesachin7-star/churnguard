01. The Problem & Industry Challenge

Customer churn is one of the highest cost drivers for subscription and SaaS businesses. Most companies react after a cancellation email arrives or an account fails to renew — when it is already too late to recover the client.

• Silent Activity Decay: Over 60% of churning clients gradually decrease seat utilization and logins weeks before cancellation without filing complaints.
• Data Heterogeneity: Different companies record usage metrics in drastically different schemas (e.g., Telecom vs. B2B Cloud vs. E-Commerce). Most legacy ML tools require months of custom data engineering for each new dataset.

02. Architectural Innovation: Universal AutoML

To address data heterogeneity, ChurnGuard AI incorporates a Universal Dataset Processor that accepts raw CSV files from any organization and automatically builds a tailored churn model:

1. Smart Schema Inference

Employs linguistic heuristics and cardinality analysis to identify Customer IDs, Target Labels, Financial Revenue, Inactivity windows, and Ticket counts.

2. Dynamic Model Training

Trains Gradient Boosting and Random Forest ensembles on the fly, performing train/test split, scaling, imputation, and feature importance weighting.

3. Root-Cause Explainability

Translates complex feature deviations into human-readable early warning reasons (e.g., "Seat utilization dropped by 45%") paired with actionable playbooks.

03. Key Features & User Experience

Interactive Risk Leaderboard

Real-time filtering by Critical, At-Risk, and Healthy tiers. Color-coded probability gauges and instant MRR-at-risk aggregation.

14-Day Activity Sparklines

Interactive Chart.js telemetry charts displaying daily active events to pinpoint the exact date when team engagement began to decline.

Targeted Retention Playbooks

One-click playbook execution (Executive Sponsor Outreach, Team Onboarding Workshops, Renewal Discounts) with audit logs.

What-If Behavioral Simulator

Interactive live sliders allowing account managers to simulate how improving seat adoption or resolving tickets reduces churn probability.
